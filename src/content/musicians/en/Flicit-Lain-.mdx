Trained in Reims with Julien Medous and then in Saint-Maur-des-Fossés with Cécile Hugonnard-Roche, Félicité Laîné is currently in her final year at the Conservatoire National Supérieur de Musique de Paris, where she studies with Frank Braley and Haruko Ueda. In 2024, she obtained her Bachelor’s degree in Piano with the highest honors from the jury.

Keen to enrich her musical practice through an openness to other disciplines, Félicité also holds a Master’s degree in Philosophy from the University of Reims, for which she wrote a thesis on the question of interpretation in music. Since 2026, she has also been pursuing a Master’s degree in Vocal Accompaniment in the classes of Anne Le Bozec and Emmanuel Olivier.

She is a prizewinner of the Île-de-France International Competition and the Virtuoses du Cœur Competition, and regularly performs in concert, primarily in France but also internationally. In 2023, she performed in Poland and Moldova in concerts with orchestra, gave a solo recital in Paris in 2024, and performed in New York and Washington, D.C. in 2025 as a prizewinner of the French-American Piano Society.

In December 2025, she made her Paris solo debut, performing a Mozart concerto with the Érable Ensemble.

In 2025, Félicité co-founded, together with five friends, an association through which they created the Les Songes Musicaux festivals, one of which takes place in her hometown.

During her studies at the Conservatoire National Supérieur de Musique de Paris, she is supported by the Kriegelstein Fund and the Patrick & Ute Petit Foundation.
