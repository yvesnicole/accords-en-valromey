Born in Aix-en-Provence in 1998, Nicolas Bourdoncle began playing the piano at a very young age. He went on to study with Roger Muraro at the Conservatoire National Supérieur de Musique de Paris, as well as with Galina Eguiazarova and Stanislav Ioudenitch at the Escuela Superior de Música Reina Sofía in Madrid, where he graduated with the highest distinctions.He also studied with Bernard d’Ascoli through the “Piano Cantabile” association, and in 2024 obtained his Artist Diploma from the Conservatoire National Supérieur de Musique de Paris.

Nicolas made his solo debut at the Odessa Philharmonic at the age of ten. Since then, he has performed in more than twenty countries as a soloist, chamber musician and with orchestras such as the Granada City Orchestra, the Leipzig Symphony Orchestra, the Baden-Baden Philharmonic Orchestra, the Romanian National Radio Orchestra, the National Philharmonic Orchestra, the National Symphony Orchestra of Ukraine, the Armenian National Philharmonic Orchestra and the Monterrey Symphony Orchestra, among others.

He has been a prizewinner at the 2012 Hanoi International Piano Competition, the 2019 Premio Jaén International Piano Competition, the 2023 Animato-Chopin Competition, the 2024 Cantù International Piano Competition, and the 2025 “Les Étoiles du Piano” Competition in Roubaix.

Nicolas Bourdoncle is the Artistic Director of the Les Songes Musicaux Festival in Châlons-en-Champagne.
